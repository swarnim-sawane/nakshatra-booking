# Nakshatra — final website copy recommendation

Date: 8 September 2026

## Verdict

The fresh source build is considerably better than the older preview: it now introduces Nilima earlier, uses customer-first FAQs, removes visible Cal ID language from the main journey, and gives the calendar a working availability state. A full rewrite is no longer necessary.

The remaining refinement should make the language simpler, reduce abstract phrases such as `clarity` and `direction`, remove repeated ideas, and speak more directly about Nilima, the customer's Kundli, and the consultation itself.

## Voice to use everywhere

- Warm, calm and personal.
- Knowledgeable without sounding grand or mystical.
- Clear enough for someone who does not know astrology terminology.
- Honest about uncertainty; no fear, guarantees or fixed outcomes.
- Booking platforms stay invisible until the external handoff.
- One idea per sentence.

## Keep unchanged

- Brand name: `Nakshatra`
- Consultation names: `Personal Consultation`, `Relationship Consultation`, `Best Date Analysis`
- Prices and durations
- Main hero heading: `Your Kundli, studied before we meet.`
- Primary action: `Book a consultation`
- The four hero proof points: experience, language, personal preparation and private format
- `Meet Nilima` as the first section after the hero

# Copy to remove completely

- Visible references to `Cal ID` in headings, buttons, FAQs and fallback messages.
- Repeated explanations of Google Meet and Razorpay outside the final booking or policy context.
- Repeated `Private consultation` labels on every booking card.
- Repeated duration labels within the same consultation card.
- `clarity and direction` when the sentence can name the customer's actual question instead.
- `What happens after the consultation?` until there is a real confirmed follow-up, notes or report policy to describe.
- Copy in currently unused components such as `CalIdHandoff`, `AboutPractice`, `HowItWorks`, `ConsultationOverview` and `TrustRow`, unless those components are intentionally brought back into the live page.

# Facts still required from Nilima

The following should not be invented:

1. Training, certification, lineage or formal astrology background.
2. Number of consultations completed.
3. Whether written notes, a recording or follow-up support are included.
4. Exact cancellation window, no-show rule and refund policy.
5. How long the external booking provider retains birth details and personal questions.
6. Genuine testimonial wording and permission to publish it.

Once confirmed, these facts should strengthen the trust section without making the homepage longer.

# Reference pattern

The recommendation follows the strongest pattern found on current practitioner sites: lead with the individual birth chart, identify who reads it, explain the experience in plain language, state confidentiality clearly, and keep scheduling mechanics secondary. It does not copy competitor wording or adopt unsupported claims.
